LEAN ✓嫁接仿射闭包（长合取的一部分）：对每个正整数 H 与嫁接组成 w，任意有限词 W（R 为 step，G 为加 w）作用在组成上都是 v ↦ step^[k] v + t，且 t 的模 H 余数属于 Lw = {a·ρ(w) + b·Mρ(w)}；step^[(H²)!] 在模 H 余数上是恒等映射；当 w 是原子块 stepʲ(1, 0) 时，两个源在一切词下的读数 gcd(q, H) 都相同，当且仅当它们模 H 同余。已冻结：Graft affine closure (part of a long conjunction): for every positive H and graft composition w, every finite word W (R = step, G = add w) acts on compositions as v ↦ step^[k] v + t with the residue of t mod H in Lw = {a·ρ(w) + b·Mρ(w)}; step^[(H²)!] is the identity on residues mod H; and when w is an atomic block stepʲ(1, 0), two sources give the same reading gcd(q, H) under every word if and only if they agree mod H. Frozen: GraftAffineClosure.result
LEAN ✓血统纤维传输：a + b ≥ 1 时，组成为 (a, b) 的有序二叉树恰有 Catalan(a + b − 1)·C(a + b, a) 棵；n 次替换把它们单射地送进 Fiber(stepⁿ(a, b))，组成与可观测量 q 随之变成 stepⁿ(a, b) 与 q(stepⁿ(a, b))；均匀分布的推前与目标纤维均匀分布的全变差为 1 − |Fiber(a, b)| / |Fiber(stepⁿ(a, b))|；a + b ≥ 2 时 1 − (1/2)ᵇ ≤ TV(1)；TV(n) → 1；且 TV((1, 1), 1) = 2/3。已冻结：Genealogical fiber transport: for a + b ≥ 1 there are exactly Catalan(a + b − 1)·C(a + b, a) ordered binary trees of composition (a, b); n substitutions send them injectively into Fiber(stepⁿ(a, b)), their composition and quantity q becoming stepⁿ(a, b) and q(stepⁿ(a, b)); the total variation between the pushforward of the uniform law and the uniform law of the target fiber is 1 − |Fiber(a, b)| / |Fiber(stepⁿ(a, b))|; for a + b ≥ 2, 1 − (1/2)ᵇ ≤ TV(1); TV(n) → 1; and TV((1, 1), 1) = 2/3. Frozen: GenealogicalFiberTransport.result

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

有限观察者只读到边界：一个组成 v = (a, b)（α 叶与 β 叶的个数）和它的可观测量 q(v) = 2a + 3b。Fibonacci 一步 step(a, b) = (b, a + b) 就是替换 α → β、β → βα 在组成上的效果。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 嫁接。词里的 R 做一步 step，G 加上嫁接组成 w。无论词怎样排，结果都等于 stepᵏ(v) 加一个平移 t（k 是 R 的个数），t 模 H 的余数落在 w 与 Mw 张成的嫁接空间 Lw 里。舞台左边是 (ℤ/H)² 的余数格：青色格是 Lw，品红圈是 stepᵏ(v)，琥珀点是当前组成，虚线是 +t。w 是原子块时 Lw 是整个格；把 H 设为 6、倍数 c 设为 2，可以看到真子格。
  2. 血统。组成为 (a, b) 的有序二叉树共有 Catalan(a + b − 1)·C(a + b, a) 棵。替换 n 代后，每棵树的组成都是 stepⁿ(a, b)、可观测量都是 q(stepⁿ(a, b))：边界读数完全相同；而替换是单射，这些树在新纤维里只占 |Fiber(a, b)| 个位置。舞台上排是祖先（第 0 代），下排是它们第 n 代的像，虚线连起每一条血统。页面沿用 Lean 的记法：组成 (a, b) = (α 叶数, β 叶数)，step(a, b) = (b, a + b)，M = [[0, 1], [1, 1]]；把 β 记作 A、α 记作 B，替换就是 A → AB、B → A，(#A, #B) 一步变为 (#A + #B, #A)，两种写法是同一件事。均匀分布推前后与新纤维上均匀分布的全变差 TV(n) = 1 − |Fiber(a, b)| / |Fiber(stepⁿ(a, b))| 趋于 1；(1, 1) 在第一代恰为 1 − 2/6 = 2/3。
  3. 亏量。把 Zeckendorf 表示的权重 Wᵢ 换成 φ^(i+2) 得到 β(v)，亏量 c(v₁, v₂) = β(v₁) + β(v₂) − β(v₁ + v₂) 在 ℤ[φ] 中精确算出：φ 的系数相消，c 是整数。在收缩面上（φ 换成 ψ = 1 − φ）三个读数都在长度为 1 的窗口 [−ψ², −ψ] 里，所以 c 落在开区间 (−1.382, 1.618) 中，只能是 −1、0、1。时间轴让 v₂ 从 0 走到 89。

A finite observer reads only the boundary: a composition v = (a, b) (the numbers of α and β leaves) and its quantity q(v) = 2a + 3b. One Fibonacci step step(a, b) = (b, a + b) is what the substitution α → β, β → βα does to a composition. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Graft. In a word, R takes one step and G adds the graft composition w. However the word is arranged, the result is stepᵏ(v) plus a translation t (k is the number of R's), and the residue of t mod H lies in the graft space Lw spanned by w and Mw. On the left of the stage is the residue grid (ℤ/H)²: cyan cells are Lw, the magenta ring is stepᵏ(v), the amber dot is the current composition, the dashed line is +t. For an atomic block, Lw is the whole grid; set H to 6 and the multiple c to 2 to see a proper subgrid.
  2. Lineage. There are Catalan(a + b − 1)·C(a + b, a) ordered binary trees of composition (a, b). After n generations of substitution every one of them has composition stepⁿ(a, b) and quantity q(stepⁿ(a, b)): identical boundary readings; and since substitution is injective, they occupy only |Fiber(a, b)| places of the new fiber. On the stage the upper row holds the ancestors (generation 0), the lower row their images at generation n, and a dashed line joins each lineage. The page keeps Lean's notation: a composition (a, b) = (number of α leaves, number of β leaves), step(a, b) = (b, a + b), M = [[0, 1], [1, 1]]; writing A for β and B for α, the substitution reads A → AB, B → A and (#A, #B) becomes (#A + #B, #A) in one step: the two notations say the same thing. The total variation between the pushforward of the uniform law and the uniform law of the new fiber, TV(n) = 1 − |Fiber(a, b)| / |Fiber(stepⁿ(a, b))|, tends to 1; for (1, 1) at the first generation it is exactly 1 − 2/6 = 2/3.
  3. Deficit. Replacing the Zeckendorf weights Wᵢ by φ^(i+2) gives β(v), and the deficit c(v₁, v₂) = β(v₁) + β(v₂) − β(v₁ + v₂) is computed exactly in ℤ[φ]: the φ-coefficients cancel, so c is an integer. On the contraction face (φ replaced by ψ = 1 − φ) the three readings lie in the window [−ψ², −ψ] of length 1, so c lies in the open interval (−1.382, 1.618) and can only be −1, 0 or 1. The rail runs v₂ from 0 to 89.
