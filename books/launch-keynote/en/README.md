## Our Epistemic Discipline: Separating Positions, Data, and Proofs

The deepest embarrassment in engineering is not having unfinished work—every real system has unfinished edges. The embarrassment is painting "done" across a blueprint that has never survived contact with reality. Richard Feynman's first principle was simple: *you must not fool yourself, and you are the easiest person to fool.* Friedrich Nietzsche would add the hammer test: tap every idol lightly, and listen for the hollow ring.

Every book in this conference edition obeys three non-negotiable truth tiers:

1. **Green `LEAN ✓` and blue zero-axiom `LEAN ✓ · Axiom-free` badges belong strictly to the Lean 4 kernel.** Whenever this edition (including Chapter 5, the Coda, and Volume III) cites a formal theorem, it resolves directly from the lockfile against a frozen declaration in `the-omega-institute/trureturing` at pinned commit [{commit}](trureturing:)—green `LEAN ✓` for standard frozen theorems and blue `LEAN ✓ · Axiom-free` for verified zero-axiom declarations (`axioms: []`, e.g., `d2_eq_zero_iff`), linking directly to the exact file and line. No green or blue checkmarks are ever hand-typed into prose.
2. **Analogies and strategic stances are labeled as analogies.** "Engine vs. vehicle," "tech tree vs. gold rush," "the tamed Red Queen," and "boundary and bulk" are sharp lenses for reasoning—positions and heuristics, **never** theorems.
3. **Red `OPEN` is reserved strictly for unsolved open problems, while engineering limits are disclosed candidly in plain sight.** Red `OPEN` blocks mark genuine unproved mathematical and scientific frontiers (such as the Extended and Grand Riemann Hypotheses, simplicity of zeros, and pair correlation—since classical RH and Dirichlet GRH were externally certified in Lean 4 in November 2026); in parallel, every unfinished engineering seam—`heca`'s pending public source release, `CMA`'s single-runtime status today, `chrono-harness`'s explicit `completeness_proven: false` specification flag, and `NyxBot` Finance operating on a demo ledger with zero external paying merchants so far—is stated with its exact scope, date, and limit in our prose and appendices.
