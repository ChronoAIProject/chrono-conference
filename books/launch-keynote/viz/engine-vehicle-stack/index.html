理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

整车的剖面：最上面是可替换的机舱，下面依次是 chrono-harness（判官）、CMA / heca（运行时）、Ornn（技能）、NyxID（金库与代理），最底下是调用最终作用的上线表面（chrono-metaverse、商户渠道）。时间轴追踪两次工具调用：先读、后写。不变的是：机舱与 NyxID 之上各层里的明文密钥永远是 0。本页是示意模型与数值演示，不是证明，也不是对线上系统的测量；“发动机与整车”是结构类比，不是定理。

  1. 能力句柄。引擎发出的调用只带一个不透明的能力句柄。数据包下行到 NyxID 时先核对作用域，再在代理内存里注入真实凭证；带着明文密钥的只有 NyxID 边界之下的那一段（代理与上线表面之间）。回程时 NyxID 先剥掉凭证相关的头，再把数据交回上面各层。
  2. 热换发动机。会话、技能版本与判官都登记在底盘里，不在引擎里。换引擎时（任何时刻都可以）会话数仍是 1，重新鉴权 0 次，正在外面跑的调用照常回来，交给新引擎。
  3. 写操作人工闸门。读调用在 NyxID 的策略检查处自动放行；写调用在“写入需人工批准”策略下停在琥珀色闸门前，直到有人按下“批准这次写入”。页面从不自动批准。改成“全部自动放行”可以对照看写调用直接穿过。
  4. 一键吊销。吊销在当前时刻发出：此后到达 NyxID 策略检查（或正在闸门前等待）的调用当场收到 403 REVOKED，不再注入凭证；运行中的沙箱在约 2.1 s 的热重载窗口内换上新的绑定，不必重启，也不必轮换上游服务的主密钥。约 2.1 s 是第二章所述 CMA 凭证热重载的量级，本页没有测量它。
  5. 时序。一格是 0.1 s，整条时间轴 10 s。读调用在 0–6 s 走完一圈，写调用 6 s 出发、6.6 s 到达闸门。这张时序表只为画面而定。

书中：第二章 · 发动机人人可买，我们造的是整车

A cutaway of the vehicle: at the top the swappable engine bay, then chrono-harness (the judges), CMA / heca (the runtime), Ornn (the skills), NyxID (the vault and proxy), and at the bottom the live surfaces the calls finally act on (chrono-metaverse and merchant channels). The rail traces two tool calls: a read, then a write. What does not move: plaintext keys in the engine bay and in every layer above NyxID stay at 0. The page is an illustrative model and a numerical illustration, not a proof, and not a measurement of the live system; "engine and vehicle" is a structural analogy, not a theorem.

  1. Capability handle. A call from the engine carries nothing but an opaque capability handle. On its way down, NyxID first checks the scope, then injects the real credential in proxy memory; only the stretch below the NyxID boundary (between the proxy and the live surface) carries the plaintext key. On the way back, NyxID strips the credential headers before handing the data to the layers above.
  2. Hot engine swap. The session, the skill versions and the judges are registered in the chassis, not in the engine. On a swap (at any moment) the session count stays 1, re-authentications stay 0, and a call that is out comes back as usual, to the new engine.
  3. Write approval gate. A read passes NyxID's policy check on its own; under "human-gate writes" a write stops at the amber gate until someone presses "approve this write". The page never approves by itself. Switch to "auto-pass all" to see the write go straight through, for comparison.
  4. Instant revocation. A revocation is issued at the current moment: from then on a call reaching NyxID's policy check (or waiting at the gate) is refused at once with 403 REVOKED and no credential is injected; running sandboxes pick up the new binding within the ~2.1 s hot-reload window, with no restart and no rotation of the upstream services' master keys. About 2.1 s is the order of the CMA credential hot reload described in Chapter 2; the page does not measure it.
  5. Timing. One tick is 0.1 s and the rail is 10 s long. The read completes its round trip in 0–6 s; the write leaves at 6 s and reaches the gate at 6.6 s. This table is chosen for the picture only.
