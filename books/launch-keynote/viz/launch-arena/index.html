理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

街角前厅（street-corner）的平面图：右侧 6 圈同心剧场共 96 + 144 + 176 + 204 + 232 + 256 = 1,108 个席位，左侧是 Donna 的接待前台、18 座编号花篮展台与小花（Blossom）看守的花店（Flower Town）。读者拖动的是到场人数与时间轴；不变的是：已入座永远不超过 1,108，同一席位永远不会锁给两个人。本页是示意模型与数值演示，不是证明，也不是线上系统的实测数据。

  1. 到场。时间轴从开场前 75 分钟（T−75）走到开场后 25 分钟（T+25），T−0 是 12 月 17 日 16:00 SGT。模型里第 k 位来宾在 T−75 之后的第 75·k/N 分钟到场，所以到第 t 分钟共到场 ⌊N·t/75⌋ 人（不超过 N）。
  2. 原子选座锁。来宾在 Donna 的前台按到场顺序逐个取锁：先找还有空位的最内圈，再从该圈的哈希位置向后探查到第一个空位并锁定。锁一次只发给一个人，所以内圈坐满之后才轮到外圈，且没有席位编号重复；页面每一帧都重新核对这一点。
  3. 满座溢流。第 1,109 位来宾起，前台不再发席位锁，而是把来宾转入同步直播：溢流序号为奇数的转到 YouTube（@ChronoAIOfficial），偶数的转到 LinkedIn Live。1,250 人到场时，1,108 人入座，142 人转入直播。
  4. 无锁竞态（对照）。小图里的虚线是一个反事实：同一分钟到场的来宾各自读取这一分钟开始时的空位表、互不加锁地抢座，于是会有两人抢到同一席位。它只用来说明锁的作用，不描述线上系统。
  5. 花篮。18 座展台按模型时间表依次摆上花篮：前 12 座每 5 分钟一座（T−70 到 T−15），后 6 座每 2 分钟一座（T−13 到 T−3）。每只花篮从花店柜台出发，经小花核验贺卡后送到自己的编号展台。

书中：第四章 · 街角前厅：一座自己运转的虚拟会场

A floor plan of the Street Corner lobby (street-corner): on the right, six concentric rings with 96 + 144 + 176 + 204 + 232 + 256 = 1,108 seats; on the left, Donna's reception desk, 18 numbered flower-basket stands and Flower Town, kept by Blossom. The reader moves the arrival load and the rail; what does not move: the seated count never exceeds 1,108, and no seat is ever locked to two guests. The page is an illustrative model and a numerical illustration, not a proof, and not data measured on the live system.

  1. Arrivals. The rail runs from 75 minutes before the keynote (T−75) to 25 minutes after it (T+25); T−0 is 16:00 SGT on 17 December. In the model, guest k arrives 75·k/N minutes after T−75, so ⌊N·t/75⌋ guests (at most N) have arrived by minute t.
  2. Atomic seat lock. Guests take the lock at Donna's desk one at a time, in order of arrival: the innermost ring with a free seat is chosen, then the lock probes forward from the guest's hash position in that ring to the first free seat and locks it. The lock is granted to one guest at a time, so an outer ring starts only when the inner rings are full and no seat number repeats; the page re-checks this on every frame.
  3. Full-house overflow. From guest 1,109 onward the desk issues no seat lock and routes the guest to the synchronized live stream: odd overflow numbers to YouTube (@ChronoAIOfficial), even ones to LinkedIn Live. With 1,250 arrivals, 1,108 are seated and 142 are routed to the stream.
  4. Naive race (for comparison). The dashed line of the small plot is a counterfactual: guests arriving in the same minute each read the free seats as they stood at the start of that minute and grab one without a lock, so two of them can take the same seat. It only shows what the lock is for; it does not describe the live system.
  5. Baskets. The 18 stands receive their baskets on a model schedule: the first 12 one every 5 minutes (T−70 to T−15), the last 6 one every 2 minutes (T−13 to T−3). Each basket leaves the Flower Town counter, passes Blossom's check of the greeting card and goes to its numbered stand.
