理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

街角前厅（street-corner）的平面图：四座悬浮玻璃看台围住前厅，北 248 + 南 248 + 东 306 + 西 306 = 1,108 个席位；看台围住的前厅里是主屏、Donna 的接待前台、小花（Blossom）看守的花店（Flower Town），以及世界地图墙前 2 排 × 9 块花篮垫位。读者拖动的是到场人数与时间轴；不变的是：已入座永远不超过 1,108，同一席位永远不会锁给两个人。本页是示意模型与数值演示，不是证明，也不是线上系统的实测数据。

  1. 看台。每座看台 8 排阶梯，中间一条过道把每排分成两半。东、西看台每排 40、40、40、38、38、38、36、36 席，南、北看台每排 32、32、32、32、30、30、30、30 席，所以四座合计逐排是 144、144、144、140、136、136、132、132。席位编号写作「看台字母 + 排号 - 座号」：N1-17 是北看台第 1 排第 17 座，E1-20 是东看台第 1 排第 20 座，W8-01 是西看台最后一排第 1 座。
  2. 到场。时间轴从开场前 75 分钟（T−75）走到开场后 25 分钟（T+25），T−0 是 12 月 17 日 16:00 SGT。模型里第 k 位来宾在 T−75 之后的第 75·k/N 分钟到场，所以到第 t 分钟共到场 ⌊N·t/75⌋ 人（不超过 N）。
  3. 原子选座锁。来宾在 Donna 的前台按到场顺序逐个取锁：先找四座看台里还有空位的最前一排，再从这一排的哈希位置向后探查到第一个空位并锁定。锁一次只发给一个人，所以第 1 排在四座看台都坐满之后才轮到第 2 排，且没有席位编号重复；页面每一帧都重新核对这一点。
  4. 满座溢流。第 1,109 位来宾起，前台不再发席位锁，而是把来宾转入同步直播：溢流序号为奇数的转到 YouTube（@ChronoAIOfficial），偶数的转到 LinkedIn Live。1,250 人到场时，1,108 人入座，142 人转入直播。
  5. 无锁竞态（对照）。小图里的虚线是一个反事实：同一分钟到场的来宾各自读取这一分钟开始时的空位表、互不加锁地抢座，于是会有两人抢到同一席位。它只用来说明锁的作用，不描述线上系统。
  6. 花篮。18 块垫位（basket-01 … basket-18）沿世界地图墙排成 2 排、每排 9 块，按模型时间表依次摆上花篮：前 12 块每 5 分钟一块（T−70 到 T−15），后 6 块每 2 分钟一块（T−13 到 T−3）。每只花篮从花店柜台出发、经小花核验贺卡，用 2.5 分钟送到自己的垫位；来宾可在 Telegram @flowerassis_bot 下单。
  7. 实测另记。2026-11-27 的全流程彩排里，Donna 处理了 13 次预约与签到，0 次掉单，中位 29 秒、最长 63 秒。这是彩排记录，本页的模型不重放它。

书中：第四章 · 街角前厅：一座自己运转的虚拟会场

A floor plan of the Street Corner lobby (street-corner): four floating glass stands enclose the lobby, North 248 + South 248 + East 306 + West 306 = 1,108 seats; inside them sit the keynote screen, Donna's reception desk, Flower Town kept by Blossom, and 2 rows × 9 basket mats along the world-map wall. The reader moves the arrival load and the rail; what does not move: the seated count never exceeds 1,108, and no seat is ever locked to two guests. The page is an illustrative model and a numerical illustration, not a proof, and not data measured on the live system.

  1. Stands. Every stand has eight tiered rows, each split in two by a centre aisle. The east and west stands seat 40, 40, 40, 38, 38, 38, 36, 36 per row, the south and north stands 32, 32, 32, 32, 30, 30, 30, 30, so the four together seat 144, 144, 144, 140, 136, 136, 132, 132 row by row. A seat code reads stand letter + row - seat: N1-17 is seat 17 of row 1 in the north stand, E1-20 seat 20 of row 1 in the east stand, W8-01 the first seat of the back row in the west stand.
  2. Arrivals. The rail runs from 75 minutes before the keynote (T−75) to 25 minutes after it (T+25); T−0 is 16:00 SGT on 17 December. In the model, guest k arrives 75·k/N minutes after T−75, so ⌊N·t/75⌋ guests (at most N) have arrived by minute t.
  3. Atomic seat lock. Guests take the lock at Donna's desk one at a time, in order of arrival: the front-most row with a free seat in any of the four stands is chosen, then the lock probes forward from the guest's hash position in that row to the first free seat and locks it. The lock is granted to one guest at a time, so row 2 opens only when row 1 is full in all four stands, and no seat code repeats; the page re-checks this on every frame.
  4. Full-house overflow. From guest 1,109 onward the desk issues no seat lock and routes the guest to the synchronized live stream: odd overflow numbers to YouTube (@ChronoAIOfficial), even ones to LinkedIn Live. With 1,250 arrivals, 1,108 are seated and 142 are routed to the stream.
  5. Naive race (for comparison). The dashed line of the small plot is a counterfactual: guests arriving in the same minute each read the free seats as they stood at the start of that minute and grab one without a lock, so two of them can take the same seat. It only shows what the lock is for; it does not describe the live system.
  6. Baskets. The 18 mats (basket-01 … basket-18) line the world-map wall in 2 rows of 9 and receive their baskets on a model schedule: the first 12 one every 5 minutes (T−70 to T−15), the last 6 one every 2 minutes (T−13 to T−3). Each basket leaves the Flower Town counter, passes Blossom's check of the greeting card and takes 2.5 minutes to reach its mat; guests order through the Telegram assistant @flowerassis_bot.
  7. Measured separately. In the 2026-11-27 full-flow rehearsal Donna handled 13 bookings and check-ins, 0 dropped, median 29 s, max 63 s. That is a rehearsal record; the model on this page does not replay it.
