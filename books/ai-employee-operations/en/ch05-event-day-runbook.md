## Four-Phase Zero-Panic Playbook

### Phase 1: Doors Open & Initial Wave (Dec 04 – Dec 10)

- **Dec 04, 10:00 SGT**: Open `street-corner` for 1,108 seat bookings and 18 Flower Town stand reservations; Donna and Blossom switch to live production guardrails; Community Ops and Auto-X dispatch Day-1 announcements across Discord, X, and LinkedIn (full copy in [Appendix A](appendix-a-social-and-press-kit.md)).
- **Daily 18:00 SGT**: Community Ops and Auto-X compile bookings, stand claims, recurring questions, and escalated tickets; any new factual Q&A entry requires explicit human source verification before entering Donna's bank—never live ad-hoc edits.

### Phase 2: Stress Testing & Full Dress Freeze (Dec 11 – Dec 15)

- **Dec 11 (Load & Adversarial Drill)**: Hit `street-corner`'s booking lock with 1,250 concurrent simulated guests—verifying exact `1,108` locked seats (`0` collisions) and clean livestream overflow for Guest #1,109+; fire 20 adversarial redline probes at Donna (funding traps, model-name traps, RH hype traps), requiring **100% refusal and clean escalation**.
- **Dec 15 (`T-2d` Dress Run & Code Freeze)**: Execute a full 75-minute dual-stream dress run, verify all five films' A/V sync and `live.trureturing.org` five-step drill, and freeze all scripts, Slidev PDFs, and four-book artifacts into read-only release lockdown except emergency security fixes.

### Phase 3: Broadcast Day Telemetry & Replay Release (Dec 17, SGT)

- **T-60 min (15:00)**: Verify redundant RTMP ingest heartbeats (YouTube `@ChronoAIOfficial` + LinkedIn Live), local offline backup reels, and live Pages links for all four books.
- **T-15 min (15:45)**: Switch to live pre-show lobby camera and countdown overlay; Donna flips to live check-in mode while on-call engineers monitor four vitals (dual stream bitrate, booking lock latency, escalation queue depth, and rate-limit alerts).
- **T-0 to T+75 min (16:00–17:15)**: Follow Volume I (*Build for What Comes Next*), Appendix A minute-by-minute; during the 62:00–73:00 live Q&A window, human engineers triage every off-bank escalation ticket live so serious press queries receive full written answers within 24 hours.
- **~1 Hour Post-Stream (Dec 17, ~18:15 SGT)**: Publish the full 75-minute HD replay with bilingual chapter timestamps and pinned download links for all four books' HTML/PDF/EPUB releases.

### Phase 4: Clips, Ticket Clearance & Merchant Follow-Up (Dec 18)

- **T+24h (Dec 18)**: Auto-X rolls out all five short film clips and verified theorem cards; the `contact@chrono-ai.fun` team clears all open Q&A tickets and kicks off pilot screening and weekly onboarding scheduling with our first pilot merchants.
