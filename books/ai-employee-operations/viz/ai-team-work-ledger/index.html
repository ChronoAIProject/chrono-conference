一块九岗运营看板：每一行是一个 AI 员工岗位，每一格是一项定义好的工作流程。青色格是截至 2026-11-27 已打通、可演示的流程，紫色格是刻意锁在人工审批闸门后或仍在路线图上的流程，两者之间的竖线就是这一岗的人类红线。本页是示意模型与数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 账本。四个分母分开说。六个小企业核心岗：Donna 14 / 15、财务 24 / 28、Auto-X 12 / 14、社群 10 / 12、HR 14 / 20、行政 8 / 10，合计 82 / 99 = 82.8%（等权平均 83.0%）。三个独立值守岗：#7 舆情监测员 6 / 7、#8 知识库维护员 3 / 3、11-27 新增的 #9 复盘分析员 11 / 11；九岗合计 102 / 120 = 85.0%（等权平均 87.1%），另外 18 项不自动化。社媒与社群手册里排班的 13 个智能体、11-26 运营审计里的 15 个在审智能体，是另外两个口径，本页不混算。HR 在严格证据审计里查出 PDF 读取的短板，如实从 15 / 20 下调到 14 / 20。财务目前只在内部与演示账本上验证，外部付费商户为 0。
  2. 闸门。来宾或记者的问题若在 30 题核验库里，Donna 带出处直接回答；不在库里、或涉及未核实的数字，Donna 不临场编造，而是记录摘要并升级到 contact@chrono-ai.fun 的人工值守队列。一笔找不到对应收据的退款同样不会自动执行，而是进入人工审批。模拟里越权承诺的计数始终为 0，这是闸门规则在模型里的结果。
  3. 倒计时。时间轴依次经过八道闸门：11-27 全流程彩排（已完成：12 段中 11 段一次通过，Donna 中位 29 s、0 丢单；唯一真故障是 Lark 内部群问答卡在网关队列 65–70 分钟，NyxID#2534，改用定时一次性任务 3.0 分钟绕过）、12-04 10:00 SGT 第一波订座开放、12-08 第二波媒体包、12-11 压测与直播演练、12-15 产物冻结、12-16 Discord 正式彩排、12-17 16:00 SGT 线上直播与 12-18 回放切片与试点跟进；各里程碑点亮哪些岗位是示意。刻度不等距：直播前后一天占得更宽。
  4. 商户起步包。先筛掉三个独立值守岗，再依次筛掉行政、HR、社群与 Auto-X，只留前台礼宾与财务台账两岗：(14 + 24) / (15 + 28) = 38 / 43 = 88.4%。这是给外部门店上手用的起步视图；截至 2026-11-27 外部付费商户仍为 0，首批试点刚刚开放。

A nine-role operations board: each row is an AI employee role and each cell a defined workflow. Cyan cells are the workflows ready and demonstrable as of 2026-11-27, violet cells those deliberately held behind a human approval gate or still on the roadmap, and the bar between them is that role's human line. The page is a schematic model and a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The ledger. Four denominators, kept apart. The six core small-business roles: Donna 14 / 15, Finance 24 / 28, Auto-X 12 / 14, Community 10 / 12, HR 14 / 20, Admin 8 / 10, together 82 / 99 = 82.8% (equal-weight mean 83.0%). Three standalone roles: #7 Sentiment Monitor 6 / 7, #8 KB Maintainer 3 / 3 and #9 Retrospective Analyst, added on Nov 27, 11 / 11; all nine together 102 / 120 = 85.0% (equal-weight mean 87.1%), with the other 18 not automated. The 13 agents scheduled in the social and community playbook and the 15 agents under the Nov 26 operational audit are two further denominators, not mixed in here. A strict evidence audit caught PDF-reading limits in HR and honestly moved it down from 15 / 20 to 14 / 20. Finance is verified on internal and demo ledgers only, with 0 external paying merchants so far.
  2. The gate. A guest's or reporter's question that is in the 30-item verified bank is answered by Donna with its source; one outside the bank, or touching unverified figures, is not improvised: Donna records a summary and escalates it to the human desk at contact@chrono-ai.fun. A refund with no matching receipt is not executed either; it goes to human approval. In the simulation the count of unauthorized commitments stays 0, which is the gate rule at work in the model.
  3. The countdown. The rail passes eight gates: the Nov 27 full-flow rehearsal (done: 11 of 12 stages passed on the first try, Donna's median 29 s with 0 dropped; the one real failure was a Lark internal-group Q&A request stuck in the gateway queue for 65–70 minutes, NyxID#2534, bypassed in 3.0 minutes by a scheduled one-shot task), the Dec 4 10:00 SGT wave 1 booking open, the Dec 8 wave 2 press kit, the Dec 11 load and stream drill, the Dec 15 artifact freeze, the Dec 16 formal Discord dress rehearsal, the Dec 17 16:00 SGT live broadcast, and the Dec 18 replay clips and pilot follow-up; which roles light up at each milestone is illustrative. The scale is not uniform: the day around the broadcast gets more room.
  4. The merchant starter pack. Filter out the three standalone roles, then Admin, HR, Community and Auto-X one after another, down to the front-desk concierge and the finance ledger: (14 + 24) / (15 + 28) = 38 / 43 = 88.4%. It is the starting view for an outside store; as of 2026-11-27 external paying merchants are still 0, and the first pilot cohort is just opening.
