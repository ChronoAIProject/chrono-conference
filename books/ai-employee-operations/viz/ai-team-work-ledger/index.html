理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

一块六岗运营看板：每一行是一位 AI 员工，每一格是一项定义好的工作流程。青色格是截至 2026-11-25 已打通、可演示的流程，紫色格是刻意锁在人工审批闸门后或仍在路线图上的流程，两者之间的竖线就是这一岗的人类红线。本页是示意模型与数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 账本。Donna 13 / 15、财务 24 / 28、Auto-X 11 / 13、社群 9 / 11、HR 15 / 20、行政 5 / 10，合计 77 / 97 = 79.4%；另外 20 项不自动化。财务目前只在内部与演示账本上验证，外部付费商户为 0。
  2. 闸门。来宾或记者的问题若在 30 题核验库里，Donna 带出处直接回答；不在库里、或涉及未核实的数字，Donna 不临场编造，而是记录摘要并升级到 contact@chrono-ai.fun 的人工值守队列。一笔找不到对应收据的退款同样不会自动执行，而是进入人工审批。模拟里越权承诺的计数始终为 0，这是闸门规则在模型里的结果。
  3. 倒计时。时间轴依次经过六道闸门：12-04 10:00 SGT 第一波订座开放、12-08 第二波媒体包、12-11 压测与演练、12-15 全程彩排与冻结、12-17 16:00 SGT 线上直播与 12-18 回放切片与试点跟进；各里程碑点亮哪些岗位是示意。刻度不等距：直播前后一天占得更宽。
  4. 商户起步包。把六岗筛到前台礼宾与财务台账两岗：(13 + 24) / (15 + 28) = 37 / 43 = 86.0%。这是给外部门店上手用的起步视图；截至 2026-11-25 还没有外部付费商户上线。

A six-role operations board: each row is an AI employee and each cell a defined workflow. Cyan cells are the workflows ready and demonstrable as of 2026-11-25, violet cells those deliberately held behind a human approval gate or still on the roadmap, and the bar between them is that role's human line. The page is a schematic model and a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The ledger. Donna 13 / 15, Finance 24 / 28, Auto-X 11 / 13, Community 9 / 11, HR 15 / 20, Admin 5 / 10, in total 77 / 97 = 79.4%; the other 20 are not automated. Finance is verified on internal and demo ledgers only, with 0 external paying merchants so far.
  2. The gate. A guest's or reporter's question that is in the 30-item verified bank is answered by Donna with its source; one outside the bank, or touching unverified figures, is not improvised: Donna records a summary and escalates it to the human desk at contact@chrono-ai.fun. A refund with no matching receipt is not executed either; it goes to human approval. In the simulation the count of unauthorized commitments stays 0, which is the gate rule at work in the model.
  3. The countdown. The rail passes six gates: the Dec 4 10:00 SGT wave 1 booking open, the Dec 8 wave 2 press kit, the Dec 11 load and rehearsal drill, the Dec 15 dress rehearsal and freeze, the Dec 17 16:00 SGT live broadcast, and the Dec 18 replay clips and pilot follow-up; which roles light up at each milestone is illustrative. The scale is not uniform: the day around the broadcast gets more room.
  4. The merchant starter pack. Filter the six roles down to the front-desk concierge and the finance ledger: (13 + 24) / (15 + 28) = 37 / 43 = 86.0%. It is the starting view for an outside store; as of 2026-11-25 no external paying merchant is live yet.
